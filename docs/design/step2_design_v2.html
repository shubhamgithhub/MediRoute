The new design

AI reads and judges messy text; code decides and checks. Same pattern as Step 1 (portal benchmark: 85% correct, 4% confidently wrong, ~3 s).

No treatment advice: rules for every step

MediRoute uses only what the patient or their doctor already said, to find hospitals that can provide it. It never decides what treatment the patient needs.

RuleWhere it is enforced
Procedures come only from the patient's text or report, never inferred ("aortic stenosis" alone gives no procedure)Step 1 evidence check (verify_phrase): a phrase not in the text is dropped
No procedure mentioned → rank by speciality, quality and location onlyCapability = "speciality only" for every hospital
The capability judge only says whether a hospital's list covers what the patient asked for; it never prefers one treatment over anotherJudge prompt + closed output (exact / related / speciality only)
A "related" match is shown as "related procedure listed, confirm with the hospital", never as the same thingExplanation templates
Fixed wording: "the procedure you mentioned"; never "you need", "best for you", "recommended for you"Explanation check (code) + template fallback
Disclaimer on every result: not a diagnosis or medical advice; a doctor at the hospital confirms any treatment planResponse (as Step 1)

What this changes in the design

  • Drop the runtime LLM capability judge (step C): on our data it is the least accurate and least safe of the three. Keep rules (or embeddings) for matching.
  • The real limit is the data: procedure lists come from an unverified team sheet with about 5 items per hospital, so every method stops near 50% recall.
  • Next AI experiment: offline website extraction (step 1) to fill and verify procedure lists, measured with the same harness: does recall rise above 50%?
  • Keep today's engine as the default; keep location tiers as an option when the area has fewer than 3 hospitals.
  • Same discipline as Step 1: AI stays only where a measured gain shows it; here the evidence says not at runtime.

Caveats: grades are LLM drafts (48% low-confidence where sites block reading); 45 requests; human check of a sample pending.
