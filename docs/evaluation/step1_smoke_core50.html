Lessons

  • What helped most: the guarded LLM judge (+16 points, 18 points fewer questions, no extra wrong answers), then the speciality boundaries (+4). Clean data fixed known mapping errors but showed no accuracy gain on these 50 cases.
  • What did not help: asking whenever the voters disagree (row 5): safe, but it asks too often unless something settles the disagreement.
  • Trade-offs found: the new data made plain search worse, and the longer prompt is slower on a CPU. Both are acceptable in the adopted design, because the LLM evidence step and the judge carry the accuracy.
  • Small sample: 50 cases give about ±12 points; the full 346-case run is in step1_benchmark_report.html.
