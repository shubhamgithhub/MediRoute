What happens to a patient's message, step by step

Three real requests sent through the running app (GPT-6 Luna on OpenRouter). Every number below comes from the app's trace for that request, or from the benchmarks in the team report.

Patient text→1 Redact→2 Red flags→3 Evidence (LLM)→3b Body areas→4 Two voters→5 Decide / ask→Step 2 hospitals

The pipeline in one table

StageWhat it doesLLM?Typical time
1 RedactRemoves e-mails, phone and ID numbers before anything else sees the textNo<1 ms
2 Red flagsFixed rules (chest pain + breathless / racing heart / dizzy / sweating, sudden loss of sight, new confusion) → Emergency Medicine at onceNo<10 ms
3 EvidenceLLM copies medical phrases word for word and tags each symptom with a body area; code keeps a phrase only if ≥ 60% of its words are in the textYes~2 s
3b Body areasCode counts body areas; 2+ areas and no named diagnosis → ask which areaNo<1 ms
4a Lookup voterKeyword + meaning search of each phrase over 10,884 medical entries (ICD-10 9,170 · CGHS procedures 1,270 · lay terms 444)No0.1–2 s
4b LLM voterReads the whole message, picks one of 26 specialities (runs in parallel with 4a)Yes~2 s
5 DecideBoth agree → answer · disagree → LLM judge or ask · nothing found → askOnly if they disagree0–2 s
Step 2Filter by speciality and city → FAISS meaning search → small quality bonuses → ranked listNo~35 ms

Likely questions, short answers

QuestionAnswer
Why not just ask the LLM for the speciality?LLM only: 76% correct, 16% confidently wrong on core50. Two independent methods that must agree cut confident errors to 2%.
Why use an LLM at all?Patients write freely ("blocked arteries", "sugar problem"). LLM-extracted phrases raise lookup accuracy from 87–88% to 93%. It only copies words; code checks each phrase.
Can the LLM invent a symptom?A phrase is kept only if ≥ 60% of its words are in the message; every check is logged on the admin page.
What if the LLM is down?Evidence falls back to code keywords and the lookup still votes; the patient sees a notice; more questions instead of guesses.
Why is "chest pain" not an emergency?The red-flag rule needs chest pain plus breathlessness, racing heart, dizziness or sweating. No false alarm on core50, mediroute or 110 unseen messages.
Why ask so often on symptom-only messages?Design choice: a question costs one answer; a confident error sends the patient to the wrong hospital. 98% are right after one answer.
What is Reciprocal Rank Fusion?Keyword and meaning search give different score scales; RRF adds 1/(60 + rank) from each, so only ranks matter. Standard method (k = 60).
How are hospitals ranked?Filter by speciality and city → FAISS cosine similarity (multi-qa-MiniLM) → bonuses for website-confirmed speciality, verification, NABH, JCI, international desk, evidence quality, matched procedures; capped at +0.20.
Is the ranking better than a search engine?Keyword search without filters puts an incapable hospital in the top 3 in 51% of requests; ours 0% (first run), NDCG@5 0.807 vs 0.50.
Who checked the labels?Team-written and public sets; a doctor review of the labelling rule and mapping is scheduled 22 Nov – 1 Dec. Results are shown under both label views.
Cost and speed?≈ $0.0004 per search, p50 2.55 s end to end; Step 2 ~35 ms, no LLM.
Privacy?Contact details redacted first; reports read in memory, never stored; saved history only with an account and consent, deleted after 90 days.
