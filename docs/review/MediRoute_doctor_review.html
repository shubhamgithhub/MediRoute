MediRoute: clinical review request

Capstone project, PG programme in AI/ML (IIT Bombay) · prepared 11 November 2026 · draft data, not for clinical use

What MediRoute does. An international patient describes their condition (often with an existing diagnosis or report). Step 1 suggests which medical speciality to search for; Step 2 lists Indian hospitals for that speciality. It is a hospital discovery tool: it does not diagnose, advise treatment or judge urgency, except for a fixed emergency warning. A doctor at the hospital always confirms the treatment plan.

Why we need you. Our evaluator asked that the diagnosis-to-speciality links and the test cases be reviewed by a clinician rather than written only by engineers. Everything below is our draft. Please mark what is wrong; you do not need to explain what is right.

  • Think "where should a hospital coordinator send this patient?", not "what is the diagnosis?".

2 Diagnosis group → speciality table (CCSR)

Priority: critical · about 1 hour · file data/processed/specialty_mapping.csv (rows with source = ccsr)

To link a diagnosis to a speciality we use AHRQ's Clinical Classifications Software Refined (CCSR), which groups every ICD-10-CM diagnosis code into about 540 clinical categories (for example all heart-failure codes → "Heart failure"). We then map each category to one of our 26 specialities. The grouping is AHRQ's; the category → speciality step is ours and needs your review. Each row also says whether a match is a diagnosis (strong evidence), a symptom (weak evidence, the system may ask a question) or a status (an encounter, e.g. chemotherapy).

Questions for you
  1. We have no Rheumatology speciality: rheumatoid arthritis, lupus and gout go to General Medicine (+ Orthopaedics). Acceptable, or should we add Rheumatology?
  2. We have no Vascular Surgery or General Surgery: varicose veins and aneurysms go to Cardiology / Cardiothoracic Surgery; hernia and gallbladder surgery to Gastroenterology. Acceptable, or should we add them?
  3. Injuries: fractures → Orthopaedics; brain and spinal cord injury → Neurosurgery; internal organ injury, wounds and poisoning → Emergency Medicine. Correct?
  4. Symptoms map to the body system they point to (e.g. throat pain and cough → Pulmonology + ENT; abdominal pain → Gastroenterology), as weak evidence. Correct groupings?
  5. Obesity → Bariatric Surgery + Endocrinology; osteoporosis → Endocrinology + Orthopaedics; urinary tract infection → Urology + General Medicine. Correct?
  6. Is any evidence type wrong (a symptom marked as a diagnosis, or the other way round)?
